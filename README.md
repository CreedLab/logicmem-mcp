LogicMem — *Prove what your AI did.* Tamper-evident records of AI agent actions and memory, built for teams in regulated fintech and insurance.

- Website: https://logicmem.io
- Package: `pip install logicmem` (https://pypi.org/project/logicmem/)

LogicMem is designed to support record-keeping duties such as EU AI Act Article 12; it is not a compliance certificate, and nothing here is legal advice.
